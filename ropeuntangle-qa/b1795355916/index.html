How to play

Tap a rope end

Every rope hangs between two holes. Tap the end you want to move — or drag it.

Drop it in an empty hole

Each move carries one end to an empty hole. Every move uses one turn.

Untangle every crossing

Red dots mark where ropes cross. Clear them all and the stage is done.

Scissors, hints and extra turns

Cut a rope, ask for a hint, or add three turns when a stage gets tight. Locked holes open once the knots thin out.
