Sort Streak

Sort every flower rope into its own pot.

How to play

Flowers hang in ropes

Every rope holds a few flowers. The flower at the very bottom is the free end.

→

Lift the bottom group

Tap a rope to lift the group of same-coloured flowers at its free end, then tap another rope to hang them there.

★★★

Fill a rope to harvest

The hanging group must match the colour at the free end, or land on an empty rope. Fill a rope with one colour and it drops into the pot.

? ⚿ ⛓ ＋

Gimmicks and boosters

Question flowers stay hidden until they reach the free end, and locked ropes or chained flowers open after a few harvests. Undo, shuffle or hang one extra rope when you get stuck.
