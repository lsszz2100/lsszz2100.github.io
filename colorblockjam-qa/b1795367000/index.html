Color Block Jam

Slide every block out through its gate.

How to play

↑

Drag a block

Press a coloured block and swipe up, down, left or right. It slides until something stops it.

Find the matching gate

Each colour has one gate on the edge. Line the block up with its gate and push it through.

Clear the way first

A block in front of another has to leave first. Work out the order and the board opens up.

⏱ 🔨 💥

Beat the clock

Every stage has a time limit. Stuck? Add 30 seconds, break a block with the hammer or clear a colour with the bomb.
