Pack Merge Sort

Drop a tray. Pour the colours. Pack six.

How to play

Take the next tray

A tray holds up to six items. Tap any empty slot on the board to drop the tray shown at the front of the queue — or drag it there.

→

Neighbours pour together

When two trays touch, the same colour runs into the tray that already holds more of it. Groups grow without you lifting a finger.

+1

Six fills a pack

A tray filled with six items of one colour packs itself and flies away, clearing its cell. Pack enough trays to clear the stage.

Do not run out of space

Trays you cannot merge stay on the board. If the board fills up, the stage is over. Shuffle the queue, clear a tray or magnet a colour to rescue it.
