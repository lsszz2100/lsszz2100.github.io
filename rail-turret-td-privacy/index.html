Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Rail Turret TD: Iron Express is a single-player offline game published by Sangseon Lee (MR.lee). This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and the choices you have.

1. Information the game stores on your device

The game keeps your progress in the app's local storage on your phone only: cleared stages, stars, coins, upgrades, unlocked train skins, badges, sound and vibration settings, language, and the date you last opened the daily crate. This data never leaves your device, is not linked to an account, and is not sent to the developer or any server.

2. No account, no personal identifiers

The game has no sign-up, no login, no profile, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, location, or precise device identifiers. There is no user-generated content and no chat.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your advertising ID (Android Ad ID), IP address, coarse device and app information, and ad interaction events. The app itself does not receive or store this data.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), a Google-certified consent form is shown on first launch, and you can review or change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android system settings.

Google's own handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and AdMob and privacy.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATELoading ads only. The game itself works fully offline.
AD_IDLets the advertising SDK use the Android advertising ID as described above.
VIBRATEShort haptic feedback on hits and combos. Can be turned off in settings.

The game blocks all web requests other than the local game files and the ad SDK. Links in the settings screen (this page and the terms) open in your browser.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It contains no chat, no purchases, and no personal data collection beyond advertising as described above. If you are a parent and believe a child has used the app, simply removing the app deletes all of its data.

6. Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all game data permanently. You can also erase progress inside the app with Settings → Reset progress. Because no data is stored on a server, there is nothing else for the developer to delete on your behalf.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

게임 진행 정보(클리어한 스테이지·별·코인·업그레이드·열차 도색·배지·소리/진동 설정·언어·일일 상자를 마지막으로 연 날짜)는 이용자의 휴대폰 안 로컬 저장소에만 보관됩니다. 이 정보는 기기 밖으로 전송되지 않으며 계정과 연결되지 않고, 개발자나 어떤 서버에도 보내지 않습니다.

3. 광고

앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 배너·전면·보상형(선택) 광고를 표시합니다. 광고 게재와 측정을 위해 Google 모바일 광고 SDK가 광고 ID(Android Ad ID), IP 주소, 대략적인 기기·앱 정보, 광고 상호작용 기록을 수집할 수 있습니다. 앱 자체는 이 정보를 받거나 저장하지 않습니다.

법령이 요구하는 지역(유럽경제지역·영국·스위스 등)에서는 첫 실행 시 Google 인증 동의 양식이 표시되며, 앱의 설정 → 광고 개인정보 설정에서 언제든지 선택을 확인·변경할 수 있습니다. Android 시스템 설정에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제할 수도 있습니다.

Google의 데이터 처리 방식은 policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites 및 AdMob 개인정보 안내에서 확인할 수 있습니다.
