Unwind the yarn. Fill every spool.

How to play

Tap a loose thread end

Every thread is stretched between two bobbins. Tap the end you want to move — or drag it.

Drop it on an empty bobbin

Each move carries one end to a free bobbin. Every move uses one turn.

Wind free threads onto their spool

When a thread crosses nothing, it glows — tap it and it winds onto its matching spool. Empty the board.
