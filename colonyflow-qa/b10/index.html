Colony Flow

Send an ant down a trail. It carries its own colour home.

How to play

Every ant carries one colour

The nest is a wall of coloured pixel blocks. An ant can only carry blocks of its own colour.

→ ›

Tap an ant, then a trail

Tap an ant in the bottom bar, then tap an arrow on the edge of the board. The ant walks in from that side along the row or column.

✕

Something always stops it

The ant eats its colour and stops at the first block of another colour. Send that colour first to open the way, or come in from another side.

↺ ? 🐜

Clear the whole nest

Your hand is fixed, so a wasted ant can strand the board. Undo is free, a hint shows the next safe move, and the colony can always send one more ant.
