Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.mrlee.neonbreach (“Neon Breach”).

In short. Neon Breach is a single-player offline game. It has no account, no login and no server of ours. Your progress is stored only on your device. The only data that leaves the app is what Google AdMob needs to serve ads.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. Neon Breach does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. The game runs entirely from files bundled inside the app.

The following is stored locally on your device only (Android WebView local storage) and is never uploaded:

  • Best score, best wave, total kills and number of runs
  • Your settings: language, sensitivity, graphics quality, sound, haptics

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no account to delete and no data for us to retrieve.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Neon Breach shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner on menu, pause and result screens, an occasional interstitial after a run ends, and optional rewarded ads that the player chooses to watch (revive, double score, unlock a weapon). Ads are served by Google. Depending on your consent and your device settings, Google may use the advertising ID and technical information such as device model, OS version, approximate region and IP address to deliver and measure ads.

  • Google’s handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms.
  • You can change or withdraw ad consent at any time in the app: Settings → Ad privacy options (Google UMP consent form).
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android: Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. Refusing them never blocks gameplay.

3. Children

Neon Breach is a science-fiction shooting game intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEUsed only by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request ads. The game itself is offline and the WebView is blocked from any network request.
AD_IDAdvertising ID access for ad delivery and measurement, as required by Google Play services.
VIBRATEOptional haptic feedback (can be turned off in Settings).

5. Security

The game content is loaded from inside the app package. File access, external navigation and any web request from the game view are blocked, and the app does not use unencrypted (HTTP) connections.

6. Data deletion

Because no personal data is sent to us, there is nothing stored on our side to delete. Deleting the app removes every piece of local progress and every setting. To stop ad personalisation, use Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

1. 게임이 직접 수집하는 정보

없습니다. 네온 브리치는 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 연락처, 사진, 위치, 마이크, 카메라를 요구하지 않습니다. 게임은 앱에 포함된 파일만으로 동작합니다.

아래 항목은 기기 안에만 저장되며(안드로이드 WebView 로컬 저장소) 외부로 전송되지 않습니다.

2. 광고(구글 AdMob)

네온 브리치는 구글 AdMob으로 광고를 표시합니다. 메뉴·일시정지·결과 화면의 배너, 전투가 끝난 뒤의 전면 광고, 그리고 이용자가 직접 선택하는 보상형 광고(부활, 점수 2배, 무기 해금)가 있습니다. 광고는 구글이 게재하며, 동의 여부와 기기 설정에 따라 광고 ID와 기기 모델·OS 버전·대략적인 지역·IP 주소 같은 기술 정보가 광고 게재와 측정에 사용될 수 있습니다.

  • 구글의 데이터 처리 방식은 Google 개인정보처리방침을 참고하세요.
  • 광고 동의는 앱 안에서 언제든 변경·철회할 수 있습니다: 설정 → 광고 개인정보 설정(구글 UMP 동의 양식).
  • 광고 ID는 안드로이드 설정 → Google → 광고에서 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다.
  • 보상형 광고는 항상 선택 사항이며, 거부해도 게임 진행에 지장이 없습니다.
