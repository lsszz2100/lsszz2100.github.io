Smash Quest

Tap the cluster. Smash the quest.

How to play

Tap a cluster

Tap two or more touching gems of the same colour to smash them. Every smash uses one move.

x5

Chain the combo

Clear 3 or more gems in a row and your combo climbs. Bigger clusters score more.

Ice and crates

Tap ice to break it. Crates split when you smash gems next to them.

Boosters

Stuck? Use a hammer, drop a bomb, or reshuffle the board. They cost no moves.
