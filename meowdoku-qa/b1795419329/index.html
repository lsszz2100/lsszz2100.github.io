Meowdoku

One cat per row, column and colour. No two cats touch.

How to play

Every colour is a room

The board is split into colour regions. Each row, each column and each colour region needs exactly one cat.

× → 🐱

Tap to mark, drag to cross out

Tap a cell to cycle between empty, × and a cat. Drag across cells to paint × quickly, and drag from an × to rub it out.

🐱 🚫 🐱

Cats never touch

Two cats may not sit next to each other, not even corner to corner. The red ring warns you the moment a rule breaks.

↺ ? ✧

When you get stuck

Undo takes back a move, a hint points out what the rules already decide, and Fix clears any cat that breaks the only solution.
