How to play

  1. Tap a vehicle whose lane to the edge is clear. Blocked ones wait until the cars in front have gone.
  2. It drives into a free boarding bay, then takes passengers of its colour from the front of the queue.
  3. Mini cars seat 4, vans seat 6, buses seat 8. A full vehicle leaves at once and frees its bay.
  4. Every bay taken and no bus matching the queue front? The stage is jammed — open one more bay to carry on.
  5. Get every rider on board to clear the stage. No timer, no rush.
