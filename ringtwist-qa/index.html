How to play

  1. Tap the left or right side of a ring to turn it one step. Beads sitting outside that ring turn with it.
  2. A bead falls outward when the next ring's gap reaches its slot. If the slot below is taken, it waits on the gap.
  3. A ring with a bead stuck on its gap cannot turn. Free the bead underneath, or open it from inside.
  4. A padlocked ring opens once you have freed enough beads. Clear the innermost beads first.
  5. Free every bead before your turns run out. No timer, no rush — but the turns are counted.
