Goods Triple Sort

Tap three of a kind. Clear the shelf.

How to play

Take goods off the shelf

Tap a product on a shelf, or drag it down to the tray. Only the front item of each spot can be taken — the ones behind it unlock as the front clears.

x3

Three of a kind sells

Every item you take goes into the tray at the bottom. The moment the tray holds three identical goods they are sold together and the tray frees up.

★★★

Clear the whole shelf

Keep selling until no goods are left on the shelves. The faster you clear a stage, the more stars you collect.

↺ ? ＋

Mind the tray

The tray holds seven goods. If it fills up before the shelf is empty, the stage ends — undo the last pick, add a slot or start again.
