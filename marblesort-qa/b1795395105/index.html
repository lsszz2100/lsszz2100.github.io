Marble Sort

Sort the marbles. Clear the rail.

How to play

☝

Lift the top marbles

Tap a rail to pick up the marbles on top. Tap it again to put them back.

Drop them on their colour

A marble only lands on an empty rail or on the same colour. Nothing else will accept it.

✓ ★★★

Fill one rail per colour

A rail filled with a single colour locks in and gets a check. Sort every marble to clear the stage.

Mind your moves
