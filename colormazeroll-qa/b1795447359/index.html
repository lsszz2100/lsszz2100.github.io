Color Maze Roll

Roll. Paint every tile.

How to play

→

Swipe to roll

Swipe any direction and the ball rolls that way until a wall stops it. Every tile it crosses is painted.

Paint every tile

A stage is cleared when every floor tile is painted. Rolling over a painted tile again is safe.

Gates and portals

A colour gate changes your paint colour. A portal takes the ball across the maze and it keeps rolling the same way.

✦ ✹ ↺

Boosters

Hint shows the next swipe, Paint Bomb splashes the tiles around the ball, and Undo takes back your last roll.
