Pipe Flow

Turn the pipes. Let the water run.

How to play

Tap to turn

Every pipe piece turns a quarter turn when you tap it. Keep turning until the pieces line up.

Reach every outlet

Water leaves the valve and must reach every outlet of its colour. Follow the colour of each line.

!

Watch for leaks

An open end that faces nothing is a leak. Every opening of a wet pipe has to meet another pipe or an outlet.
