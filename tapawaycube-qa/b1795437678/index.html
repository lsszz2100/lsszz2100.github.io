Tap Away Cube

How to play

→

Tap an arrow

Every block carries an arrow. Tap it and the block flies out that way. One tap costs one move.

→ ← !

Watch the path

If another block stands in the way, the block bounces back and your move is wasted. Look along the arrow first.

→ ↗ x2

Tough blocks

Blocks with bolts need two clean taps: the first cracks them, the second sends them flying.

↺ ⟳ ✹

Boosters

Undo a tap, rotate an arrow, or blow a block away. Boosters never cost a move.
