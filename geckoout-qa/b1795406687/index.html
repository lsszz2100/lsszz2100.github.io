Gecko Out

Slide every gecko out through its own gate.

How to play

Each gecko is a chain

A gecko is a chain of body segments, and its coloured gate waits on the edge of the board.

→

Drag the head or the tail

Drag the head to walk forward, or drag the tail to back the gecko up. The whole body follows, bending one cell at a time.

★★★

Slide the head into the gate

When the head reaches the gate of the same colour the gecko slips out. Free every gecko before the timer runs out.

❄ → ⚿ ⏱

Gimmicks and boosters

Ice cracks after two bumps, arrows are one-way lanes, and a locked gate opens once its key is picked up. Freeze time, undo a slide or take a hint when you are stuck.
