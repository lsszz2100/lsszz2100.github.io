How to play

  1. Tap a bolt that no plate covers. It spins out and flies to the box of its colour.
  2. Three bolts fill a box. A full box closes, scores, and the next colour moves in.
  3. A bolt with no matching box waits in the tray. Five slots, so watch the space.
  4. The last bolt out drops the plate and reveals what was underneath.
  5. Clear every bolt to finish the stage. No timer, no rush.
