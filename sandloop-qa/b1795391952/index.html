Sand Loop

Pour the falling sand into the right jar.

How to play

Sand keeps falling

Coloured sand slides down the rail. The scoop waiting at the spout is the next one you must place.

Pour it into the right jar

Each jar shows its target layers as a faint outline. Tap a jar whose next layer matches the sand colour and the sand pours in.

★★★

Fill every jar

Wrong colours bounce off a jar. Fill every layer of every jar and the stage is cleared — the faster you pour, the more stars you get.

Boosters and spare shelves

Magnet pours the next scoops for you, shuffle remixes the falling sand, undo steps back, and hint points at the right jar. Two spare shelves can hold sand you want to place later.
